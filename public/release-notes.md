# Political Atlas · 2026-09-05

The 42-question public compass is becoming a political atlas: a 30-question core
and optional chapters, with 83 available propositions in total. The manuscript
identity remains.

## Changes

- Separate position, uncertainty, mixed views, importance and certainty.
- Explicit independent scoring for economics, social values, checks on power,
  civil liberties, building policy and narrower trust/foreign/technology facets.
- Reworded bundled and loaded propositions get new identifiers. Old uncertain
  answers remain uncertain; old coordinates are not silently reinterpreted.
- Source-backed figure comparisons show coverage and item-level rationales.
  Unknown or inferred positions do not become neutral answers.
- Replace inconsistency accusations with context prompts, and remove claims
  about what the public believes or how much of someone's ideology is explained.
- Add a private versioned notebook, local voluntary comparison files, a
  disagreement guide, reasons, hypothetical tradeoff exploration and PNG export.
- Add race-specific Minnesota records and retain unscored candidates visibly.
- Add public evidence challenge drafts, submitted only by the user through GitHub.
- Retain historical faction territory context and version-separate ledger entries.
- Add an interpretation-pilot workflow and methods explanation.

## Limitations

This is not a statistically validated instrument. No human interpretation pilot,
representative sample, reliability study or calibrated confidence interval is
claimed. More questions or more precise numbers do not establish validity.

The full 69-figure evidence inventory includes many unknown or inferred items.
Review dates describe the inventory, not universal fresh verification. Only
supported exact-question positions enter current comparisons. The earlier
estimates and original dossiers are retained in the source repository.

Minnesota race records combine official primary results and filing records.
The final general-election ballot export was not independently verified. Check
the official ballot link in the app; unscored candidates are not excluded.

Dated current-affairs questions have review dates and do not alter durable
scores. Personal histories compare identical wording and retain their instrument
and scoring versions. A change of model is not reported as a change of mind.

## Privacy

Your survey, reasons, reflections and notebook are local. A comparison export
includes political answers, importance and certainty only after an explicit
checkbox; private reasons and notes are excluded. Notebook backups contain notes.
Neither imports nor exports automatically submit anything to another person.

The optional public ledger publishes a chosen name, two coordinates, an instrument
identifier and time. Its records are create-only. No raw answers are sent.
