# Election evidence additions: September 5, 2026

This is an exact-item review against `src/instrument.js`, not a new comprehensive rating of six candidates. `docs/archive/research-2026-09/election-evidence-additions.json` is an additive review artifact; the main evidence file was not edited. Unknown entries identify rejected legacy mappings and must remain unscored. Inferred entries expose the additional interpretation needed and must not enter documented-only coverage. Publication dates are null when not provided by the inspected source; September 5 is the review date, not an invented publication date.

The requested goal of six shared core positions per candidate was not supported by the available evidence. Do not relax the comparison threshold or inflate coverage to compensate. Candidate lists can still show documented individual positions and explain insufficient overlap.

## Sources inspected

- [Flanagan campaign priorities](https://peggyflanagan.com/priorities/): direct policy statements across healthcare, wages, labor, reproductive access, childcare, trade, foreign policy and housing. Targeted student relief cannot establish blanket cancellation; national paid leave does not specify federal financing. Broad equality commitments cannot establish sports eligibility or identity-document categories.

Generic tax, health-bureaucracy, and voter-ID rhetoric cannot supply answers about wealth taxes, a universal insurance guarantee, unions, or trust in vote counting.

Statements about visa additions and compliance with court orders support the revised/current questions directly.
- [Demuth campaign priorities](https://lisaformn.com/priorities/) and [affordability agenda](https://lisaformn.com/affordability-agenda/): nuclear development is explicit; corporate-tax benefits and classroom ideology require inference to the exact wording. Her [March 4, 2025 legislative release](https://www.house.mn.gov/members/profile/news/15498/50258) directly establishes sports eligibility policy. That policy supplies no evidence for medical treatment or document categories.
- Finstad's [campaign issues](https://finstadforcongress.com/issues/) and [office education page](https://finstad.house.gov/issues/education/) were checked. [GovInfo H.R. 28](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/BILLS-119hr28ih) establishes his original cosponsorship of the sports restriction. [House Clerk roll call 151](https://clerk.house.gov/Votes/2024151) records his April 20, 2024 vote against the Ukraine supplemental. The vote is a historical action, not proof of opposition to every potential aid package.
- [Johnson campaign priorities](https://www.jakejohnsonforcongress.com/priorities): explicit tariffs and housing priorities. His budget pledge supports only an inferred answer to the precise deficit-versus-tax-cuts tradeoff. Healthcare-program protection is not a universal guarantee; agricultural consolidation is not technology-company breakup. Being a union leader cannot by itself answer the union-benefit question.

Rationale text distinguishes freshly inspected sources from retained dossier material and from inferences. Additions contain both improved evidence and explicit corrections; merge at item level, with these reviewed unknowns superseding unsupported legacy entries. Keep provenance text visible in the evidence panel. Six shared documented answers were not achieved across any of the three opposing candidate pairs.
