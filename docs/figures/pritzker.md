# JB Pritzker - evidence dossier
Updated: 2026-08-11 · Researcher: session:opus-compass-modes-20260811

Illinois governor, Hyatt heir, and the confrontation wing of the Democratic
bench. He refused the demand to call up the Illinois National Guard for federal
deployment into Chicago, called the deployment outrageous and un-American,
litigated it, and got mocked personally by the White House for it. Progressive
across every issue axis, funded his own campaigns, and is the one Democratic
governor here who explicitly rejects the both-sides-can-work-together framing
that Whitmer, Shapiro and Beshear all use.
