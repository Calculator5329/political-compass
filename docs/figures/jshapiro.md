# Josh Shapiro - evidence dossier
Updated: 2026-08-11 · Researcher: session:opus-compass-modes-20260811

Pennsylvania governor, former state attorney general, running for a second term
in a state Trump carried, and openly interested in "being part of that
conversation" about 2028. The method is deal-making: he went to the Trump White
House to press on electricity capacity and PJM pricing in the same season he
said the tariff program was looking to screw over Pennsylvania farmers. Sits
with Whitmer and Beshear on the cooperative side of the Democratic bench, well
away from Pritzker.
