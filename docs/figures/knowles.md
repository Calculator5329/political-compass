# Michael Knowles - evidence dossier
Updated: 2026-08-11 · Researcher: session:opus-compass-modes-20260811

Daily Wire host since 2016 and the most prominent Catholic voice in American
conservative media, who argues in 2026 that the Constitution encodes Aquinas'
mixed regime and a natural-law tradition despite the founders' Protestantism,
and who counts the result approvingly: Catholics are 20% of the population,
28% of Congress, 38% of governors, two thirds of the Supreme Court, the vice
president, and every viable 2028 candidate in both parties.

The useful contrast on this roster is with Prager. Both are hard right on the
issues and both argue from religion, but Prager wants the liberal order
defended and Knowles does not think a neutral liberal order was ever real or
worth defending. That is what puts him insurgent on the system axis while his
economics stay conventional.
