# Amy Klobuchar - evidence dossier
Updated: 2026-08-11 · Researcher: session:opus-compass-modes-20260811

Four-term Minnesota senator, now the front-runner for governor after Walz's
exit. Her placement is set by two things: a conventional Democratic issue
profile with moderate edges (public option rather than single payer, no wealth
tax, prosecutor's instincts on crime), and a strongly institutionalist system
axis. She is the Senate's leading tech-antitrust author, a hard no on broad
tariffs, and loud against Operation Metro Surge without joining calls to
abolish ICE.

> Evidence density: this is a first-pass dossier written from campaign and
> office sources listed below plus her documented Senate record, not the
> per-item deep-research pass the 2026-07-18 national cohort received. Items
> marked [record] rest on her long-standing, uncontested public position
> rather than a cite gathered for this pass. A verification lane should
> confirm those before the roster is treated as settled.
