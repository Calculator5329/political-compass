# Dave Ramsey - evidence dossier
Updated: 2026-08-11 · Researcher: session:opus-compass-modes-20260811

Personal-finance broadcaster rather than a political commentator, on the board
because the audience is enormous and the economics are a politics: individual
responsibility over government programs, churches and communities rather than
the safety net, and the loudest sustained argument in American media against
student-debt cancellation. He rejects the framing directly, saying people have
lost the ability to think because of political pigeonholing, and much of the
instrument finds nothing to score as a result. That is not evasion on his part
or on the instrument's: he is a Christian personal-finance teacher who is
economically hard right, socially conventional, and has no system-axis politics
at all.

> Evidence density: first-pass dossier, and the second-thinnest on the board
> after PF Jung. The economic items rest on decades of consistent on-air
> teaching; the social ones on his explicit Christian framing; the system items
> are almost entirely 0 under the METHOD rule because there is nothing to score.
> Items marked [record] rest on his uncontested public line.
