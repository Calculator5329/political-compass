# Lisa Demuth - evidence dossier
Updated: 2026-08-11 · Researcher: session:opus-compass-modes-20260811

Speaker of the Minnesota House, elected 67-65 on party lines in February 2025,
and the first Black person and first Republican woman to hold the gavel.
Running for governor as the competence candidate against the Walz record:
lower taxes, tougher on crime, end the fraud, repeal the nuclear moratorium,
protect girls' sports. Conservative on the issues and institutional in method,
having governed through a tied chamber, with one insurgent note in staying in
the race after the party endorsement over convention voting-machine
irregularities.
