# Josh Hawley - evidence dossier
Updated: 2026-08-11 · Researcher: session:opus-compass-modes-20260811

Missouri senator and the Senate's working experiment in right-wing economic
populism: he co-sponsored a federal minimum-wage increase with a Democrat,
walks picket lines, wrote the book against the tech platforms, published an
op-ed telling his own party not to cut Medicaid, and then, days after voting
for the megabill that cut it, introduced a bill to repeal that provision. The
heterodoxy is real and it is narrow. On the social and system axes he is as
hard right as anyone on the board, including the objection to certifying the
2020 election, which is why the economics barely move his placement left.
