# Glenn Youngkin - evidence dossier
Updated: 2026-08-11 · Researcher: session:opus-compass-modes-20260811

Carlyle Group co-chief executive who won Virginia in 2021 on parents' rights
and school curriculum, governed as a corporate tax-cutter, and left office in
January 2026 under the state's single-term limit. Day-one executive orders
against critical race theory and school mask mandates; a grocery-tax repeal
delivered; a fifteen-week abortion limit he pushed and could not pass. The
unresolved item is elections, where he spent four years speaking to the Trump
and the never-Trump halves of his party without ever settling which one he was
in.

> marked [record] rest on his uncontested governing record rather than a cite
> gathered for this pass. His post-gubernatorial positioning is still forming
> and this should be re-scored if he enters the 2028 race.
