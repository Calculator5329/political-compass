# Dennis Prager - evidence dossier
Updated: 2026-08-11 · Researcher: session:opus-compass-modes-20260811

PragerU co-founder and the oldest political voice in this batch, whose politics
predate the online right entirely: Reagan-era fusionism, a Judeo-Christian
civilizational argument, and an opposition to the left framed as a defense of
Western values rather than as a nationalist program. Paralyzed from the neck
down after a 2024 fall, he was still following the news and recording health
updates through June 2026. Hard right on the issue axes and, unusually for this
roster, institutionalist on the system axis: he wants the old order defended,
not replaced.

> Evidence density: first-pass dossier. His positions are stated across four
> decades of radio and hundreds of PragerU videos rather than in any one place;
> items marked [record] rest on that consistent line rather than a cite
> gathered for this pass. The recent items are the weakest, because he has been
> off the air recovering since 2024 and the current-events sources here are
> health updates (1)(2)(4)(5).
