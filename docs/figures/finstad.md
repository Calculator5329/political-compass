# Brad Finstad - evidence dossier
Updated: 2026-08-11 · Researcher: session:opus-compass-modes-20260811

Third-term First District Republican, former state representative and USDA
state director, facing Jake Johnson in November. His record is party-line with
a farm overlay: he backed the tariffs that hurt his own soybean growers, voted
against every Ukraine aid package while voting Israel aid through, calls
himself 100% pro-life, and wants the agricultural guest-worker system replaced.
Institutional in style rather than insurgent, and the farm bill he keeps
promising has not arrived in his three terms.
