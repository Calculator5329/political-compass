# Brian Kemp - evidence dossier
Updated: 2026-08-11 · Researcher: session:opus-compass-modes-20260811

Two-term governor of Georgia, and the clearest case on the board of a hard-right
issue record attached to a hard-institutionalist system record. He signed the
six-week abortion law and permitless carry, and has refused Medicaid expansion
through both terms. He also refused to call the special session that would have
overturned Georgia's 2020 result, survived the primary challenge Trump recruited
against him, won reelection by more than the man who beat him in the primary
endorsement fight, and is now steering the state's next cycle from a position
Trump had to accept.
