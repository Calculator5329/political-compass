# Andy Beshear - evidence dossier
Updated: 2026-08-11 · Researcher: session:opus-compass-modes-20260811

Twice-elected Democratic governor of a state Trump won by thirty points, chair
of the Democratic Governors Association, and the bench's argument that the party
wins by being ordinary. He vetoed the transgender youth care ban and the
abortion restrictions his legislature overrode, called the deployment of troops
to American cities un-American and joined the legal action against it, and
otherwise governs by working with the Republican supermajorities that hold both
chambers. The most moderate Democrat on the bench, and among the most
institutionalist figures on the whole board.
