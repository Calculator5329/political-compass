# Jake Johnson - evidence dossier
Updated: 2026-08-11 · Researcher: session:opus-compass-modes-20260811

Mayo High School math teacher, twenty-year union leader, and the DFL nominee
against Finstad in a red First District. Raised one of eleven children in
Stewartville; entered politics in 2024 organizing a school-funding referendum.
Runs a small-dollar campaign on costs, corruption, and consolidation: break the
buyers who set farm prices, restore the export markets the tariffs gutted,
defend Social Security and Medicare. Rural-Democratic on guns, supporting the
right while backing background checks and red-flag laws.
