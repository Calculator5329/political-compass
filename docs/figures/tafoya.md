Updated: 2026-08-11 · Researcher: session:opus-compass-modes-20260811

Thirty-year sports broadcaster, most of it on NBC Sunday Night Football, who
retired in 2022 into conservative commentary and won the Republican Senate
nomination on 2026-08-11 with 51.9% against Adam Schwarze and three others.

attacking Minnesota Democrats over safety-net fraud. Heterodox where it counts
against the activist base: pro-choice to twelve weeks, and on record in 2022
hoping Trump would not run again, which she has since replaced with a full
endorsement and more than forty donations.
