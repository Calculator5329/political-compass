# PF Jung - evidence dossier
Updated: 2026-08-11 · Researcher: session:opus-compass-modes-20260811

Long-form YouTube commentator who calls himself a radical centrist and an
applied sociologist, and whose actual subject is the political conflict rather
than any policy inside it: what the factions are, how memes move between them,
why institutions lose legitimacy. That produces the flattest answer sheet on
the board. Under METHOD's rule that 0 means genuinely mixed or no findable
evidence, most of the instrument returns 0 for him, and the placement lands
near the origin with a small insurgent lean from the procedural items.

> from adversarial sources (4)(5) rather than from statements of his own, and
> this dossier deliberately does not score from them. Treat the placement as
