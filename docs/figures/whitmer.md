# Gretchen Whitmer - evidence dossier
Updated: 2026-08-11 · Researcher: session:opus-compass-modes-20260811

Michigan governor, term-limited in 2026, who took the opposite bet from
Pritzker on how a Democrat survives this administration: repeated Oval Office
meetings to press for tariff carve-outs and Medicaid money, an appointment to
the Council of Governors, and rare public praise from Trump in return, which
much of her party read as a trap. "I'm not against tariffs outright, but it is
a blunt tool." Standard Democratic issue positions with an auto-state accent,
and the most cooperative system-axis record on the Democratic bench.
