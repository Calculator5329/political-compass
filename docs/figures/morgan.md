# Piers Morgan - evidence dossier
Updated: 2026-08-11 · Researcher: session:opus-compass-modes-20260811

British tabloid editor turned American cable host turned independent
interviewer, now running Uncensored past a billion YouTube views, four million
subscribers and a 2026 TIME Studios partnership. He is scored as what he is
rather than as what his guest list suggests: an old-media centrist whose
American career ended over his gun-control advocacy, who defends public health
coverage as unremarkable because he is British, and who has spent the past
several years making his living on the anti-woke side of the culture war while
platforming everyone in it. Institutionalist on the system axis, with a
free-speech exception that is also his business model.
