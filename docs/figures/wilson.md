# Andrew Wilson - evidence dossier
Updated: 2026-08-11 · Researcher: session:opus-compass-modes-20260811

Host of The Crucible and proprietor of Debate University, an Orthodox convert
since 2021 who argues that objective morality and truth require God and that
secular ethics collapses into nihilism. The record is thousands of debates and
viral confrontations rather than any policy history, which produces a lopsided
sheet: the social, religious and gender items are dense and confident, the
economic ones are nearly empty. His politics are a theology first and a program
second, and the program is Christian restoration rather than any settled
position on tax rates.
