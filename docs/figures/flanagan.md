# Peggy Flanagan - evidence dossier
Updated: 2026-08-11 · Researcher: session:opus-compass-modes-20260811

Lieutenant governor since 2019, member of the White Earth Band of Ojibwe, and
the highest-ranking Native woman in American executive office. Running to
Craig's left with Sanders and Ocasio-Cortez behind her: Medicare for All, a $17
minimum wage indexed to inflation, and the flat statement that Israel is
committing genocide in Gaza. Movement-left on issues, but she has spent seven
years inside the executive branch, so her system-axis scores sit closer to the
institutionalist side than her issue profile suggests.
