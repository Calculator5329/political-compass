# Keith Ellison - evidence dossier
Updated: 2026-08-11 · Researcher: session:opus-compass-modes-20260811

Attorney general since 2019, former six-term congressman and DNC deputy chair,
and by his own count the plaintiff against the Trump administration more than
fifty times: the tariff case he won at the Supreme Court in February 2026, the
suit to halt Operation Metro Surge, and the challenge to HHS conditioning
federal money on a two-sex definition. Movement-left on economics and civil
rights while running the state's law office, which is why he reads as a
left-insurgent who nonetheless litigates rather than agitates.
