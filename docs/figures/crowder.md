# Steven Crowder - evidence dossier
Updated: 2026-08-11 · Researcher: session:opus-compass-modes-20260811

Louder with Crowder, the longest-running daily conservative comedy show on the
platforms, built on the Change My Mind table and on a fusionist frame the show
states outright: limited government, free markets, individual liberties,
traditional values. The libertarianism is real on the economic items and
disappears on the social ones, which is the standard shape of the online right
and the reason his placement lands well right and moderately insurgent rather
than at the libertarian corner the show's self-description implies.
