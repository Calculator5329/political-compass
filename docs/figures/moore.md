Updated: 2026-08-11 · Researcher: session:opus-compass-modes-20260811

executive, and the bench member who keeps saying he is not running: a statewide
service-year program, mass pardons for more than 175,000 marijuana convictions,
a child-poverty agenda, and a flat "I'm not running" in 2026. Progressive on
every issue axis and conventional on the system axis, which is an unusual
combination on this board and puts him near Moore's own framing of himself as a
governor rather than an opposition figure.
